Hi, I'm David GG. I like building web products that feel solid: clear architecture, maintainable code, thoughtful user flows, and teams that can keep moving without fighting their tools.

I work as a software engineering lead focused on TypeScript, frontend systems, and technical direction. These days I'm at Oracle, building modern web applications for healthcare and cloud engineering teams. Before that, I spent a decade at MarsBased and Sngular, two of Spain's leading software consultancies, where I worked across a wide range of clients, industries, and product teams. Along the way, I've also worked with high-growth startups like Sweetspot Intelligence and MeetPays.

I care about the boring things that make software good over time: readable code, good testing habits, accessibility, useful documentation, and technical decisions that fit the team.

Outside work, I'm usually with my family, taking photos, reading, or playing videogames.
