iTEC Directory

Master's final project

Usage analytics and web dashboards for iTEC Directory.

The work was a collaboration with iTEC, a European project focused on the future classroom. It addressed a practical need: understanding how people used the directory and how its participants were connected.

What I Built

A durable system for collecting and understanding usage data.

I researched usage-data standards and web visualization techniques, then built a system for collecting, storing, and querying the data. Two dashboards presented usage and social data, making activity and relationships within the directory easier to understand.

Project Impact

Research that became part of the working iTEC Directory.

The system and dashboards became part of iTEC Directory. Through this work, the University of Oviedo also became an associated partner of the wider European project.
